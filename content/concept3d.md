### Option 2: Refined Claymation Diorama (Version 2)
*(Incorporating all feedback: level water, centered top nav, true palm-tree logo, single-line banner)*

<img src="assets/images/claymation_v2.jpg" alt="Refined 3D Claymation Diorama" style="width: 100%; max-width: 680px; border-radius: 8px; box-shadow: 0 10px 25px rgba(0,0,0,0.45); margin: 20px auto; display: block;" />

#### Key Enhancements in Version 2:
1. **Level Waterline**: The ocean waves now stretch across the entire width on one even, continuous level. Both the cute yellow whale and the sailboat float at the exact same waterline height.
2. **True Kurt's English Logo**: The palm tree is positioned on the **left** with natural coconuts and green fronds, wrapping over the horizontal blue oval with its signature **orange border** and bold white lettering ("KURT'S" on top, "English" below).
3. **Centered Top Navigation**: The glossy blue pill navigation bar is centered flush with the very top edge, opening up generous vertical room for the illustrated diorama.
4. **Single-Line Banner**: "Learning English is Fun!" is now a compact, single-row white placard with colorful playful letters, keeping the scene airy and uncluttered.
