# Refined 3D Claymation Diorama (Version 2)

Based on your feedback, here is the updated, refined Claymation concept:

![Refined 3D Claymation Diorama](claymation_v2.jpg)

### What Was Fixed:
1. **Level Waterline**: The ocean waves now run across the entire scene on one continuous, level surface. The baby yellow whale on the left and the wooden toy sailboat on the right sit on the exact same water line.
2. **True Logo Anatomy**: The palm tree is positioned on the **left**, curving over the horizontal blue oval with its signature **orange border**, and clean bold white lettering ("KURT'S" on top, "English" below).
3. **Centered Top Navigation**: The glossy blue navigation ribbon is positioned flush at the very top edge and centered, leaving maximum vertical height for the scene.
4. **Single-Line Banner**: "Learning English is Fun!" is formatted as a single horizontal line, keeping the layout balanced and airy.
